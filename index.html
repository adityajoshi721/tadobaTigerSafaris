Can I customize a trip plan?+

Yes. The duration, stay, meal plan and safari sessions can be discussed around your dates and group.

How do I send an enquiry?+

Complete the form above. It opens a prepared message in WhatsApp; press Send there to reach the team. You can also call +91 86682 00472.

Are safari sightings guaranteed?+

No. Tadoba is a wild forest and animal sightings depend on nature.

When is my booking confirmed?+

After the team confirms availability, final pricing and inclusions with you. Sending an enquiry alone does not reserve a safari or room.
